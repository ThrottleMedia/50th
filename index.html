To Our Mom

Dear Mom,

Happy 50th Birthday to the most amazing Mom.

We don't always say it enough, but we want you to know how incredibly much we appreciate you.

You have always been there for us. No matter what we need, you are there to help. No matter what happens, you support us. And no matter what, you love us.

Your kindness is something we will never take for granted. You have such a caring heart, and you are always willing to help the people you love.

What makes your love so special is that it has never depended on us being perfect. You have loved us through our best days and our difficult days. You have loved us when we made you proud, and you have loved us when we probably tested your patience.

That unconditional love is one of the greatest gifts you have ever given us.

We appreciate every sacrifice you have made, every time you have helped us, every piece of advice, every hug, every laugh, every conversation, and every little thing you do that sometimes goes unnoticed.

We love and adore you more than we could ever put into words. We are incredibly grateful that we get to call you our Mom.

We hope your 50th birthday reminds you of just how special you are to all of us.

You deserve all the happiness, love and beautiful moments that this next chapter of your life has to offer.

Thank you for simply being you.

We love you endlessly. ❤️
